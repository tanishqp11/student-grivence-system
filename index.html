How it works

How a grievance moves

Nothing gets lost in an inbox. Each ticket has an owner, a status and a written record.

1

Create your account

Register once with your roll number, department and email. Those details are reused on every ticket.

2

File the grievance

Choose a category, describe what happened, mark the priority and add the place or office involved.

3

Get a tracking ID

The portal issues a code such as GRV-2026-AB12C. Use it any time, even without signing in.

4

Follow every action

See routing, assignment, remarks and closure on one timeline. Unresolved tickets escalate after seven days.

Questions

Before you file

Who can file a grievance?

Any currently enrolled student of the university with a valid roll number. Create an account with your roll number, department and university email to submit your first grievance.

Will my grievance be anonymous?

Your name and roll number are attached to the ticket so the cell can verify facts and follow up. Only you and the grievance cell can see your tickets. Other students cannot open them, and the public tracking page shows the subject and timeline but never your personal details.

How long does a grievance take?

You get an acknowledgement and tracking ID immediately. The cell aims to record a first response within two working days and close the grievance within ten working days. If nothing moves for seven days, the ticket is flagged for escalation to the dean of student welfare.

Can I track a grievance without signing in?

Yes. Keep the tracking ID from your acknowledgement and enter it on the track status page. The ID is the only thing needed, so share it carefully.

What happens with ragging or harassment complaints?

These are marked as urgent and routed to the anti-ragging / internal complaints committee. Timelines are shorter than standard grievances, and the record is restricted to committee members and the grievance cell. If you are in immediate danger, contact campus security first.

I am not satisfied with the closure. Can I reopen it?

Reply with new information in the same grievance and the cell can reopen it, or escalate the matter in writing to the dean of student welfare with your tracking ID. The full timeline stays attached as evidence.

Something not right on campus?

Put it on record. You will get a tracking ID and can follow every update.
