Reading the timeline

What each status means

  • Pending: logged and waiting for the first review by the grievance cell.
  • In review: routed to a department or officer and being investigated.
  • Resolved: action was taken; the remark explains what was done.
  • Closed: not actionable for the reason recorded in the remark.
